tx40_datasheet.pdf

1. Overview

The TX-40 is a digital temperature sensor in an 8-pin SOIC package. It reports readings over I2C at up to 400 kHz and draws very little current between conversions.

2. Absolute maximum ratings

Stresses beyond these ratings may permanently damage the device. Functional operation is only implied under the recommended conditions in section 3.

3. Recommended operating conditions

Supply voltage: 1.8 V to 5.5 V. Operating temperature: −40 °C to 125 °C.

4. Accuracy

Typical accuracy is ±0.5 °C from 0 °C to 70 °C after calibration at the factory.
